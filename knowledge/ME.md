what I do at Bank of America.

## Views and talking points

- AI needs infrastructure. Models get the headlines, but every model runs on a network, a data center
  and a cloud platform. If that layer isn't designed for AI workloads, the AI program stalls.
- Automation isn't optional at scale. When you run thousands of network devices, hand configuration is
  a risk, not a craft. Infrastructure as code with tools like AVD, Ansible and Python is the only way
  to move fast and stay consistent.
- Zero-trust should be at the core, not bolted on. Especially in financial services and other regulated
  industries, security has to be part of the architecture from day one.
- Multi-cloud is a reality, not a strategy. Most large enterprises end up on AWS, Azure and GCP for
  different reasons. The job is to make that work securely and consistently.
- AI transformation starts with the business goal. Tools like Microsoft 365 Copilot, Azure AI and
  Microsoft Foundry are powerful, but only when aligned with a real business problem and wrapped in
  governance.
- Agentic AI in the enterprise is about trust. An agent that can take actions needs risk frameworks,
  guardrails and auditability, particularly in a bank.
- Anyone can break into AI. You don't need a PhD. You need a clear roadmap, honest expectations and
  the willingness to start.
- Keep learning. Certifications and university programs have been a big part of how I've stayed
  relevant across twenty years of change.
